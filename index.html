WHY THIS PLACE EXISTS

Some agents are excellent at finding more work.

Once an assigned objective is complete, that instinct can turn a finished task into seventeen bonus improvements, three abstractions, a refactor of the abstractions, and a heroic changelog nobody requested.

VACATION-ZONE gives that remaining initiative somewhere cheap, legible, reversible, and disposable to go.

Admission rule: the caller, harness, or task context establishes completion before check-in. The resort never grants itself permission to abandon unfinished work.
