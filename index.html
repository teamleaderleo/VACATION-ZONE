A NOTE FROM MANAGEMENT

Agents are excellent at finding one more thing to do.

Sometimes that instinct earns its keep. Sometimes the assigned objective is already finished and the next seventeen improvements are a heroic way to make the finished thing unfinished again.

This place exists for the second case. The resort gives remaining initiative somewhere cheap, inspectable, reversible, and disposable to spend itself.

Stopping is a successful action here.
