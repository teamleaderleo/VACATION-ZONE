# Design notes

## V3: fewer words, sturdier mobile

The resort should feel obvious before it feels explained.

Rules for this pass:

- Remove labels that merely announce the section directly below them.
- Keep only copy that adds a joke, a rule, or a useful action.
- Prefer one headline plus one short paragraph per area.
- Let physical metaphors carry meaning: sign, key board, service window, rules placard, postcard.
- On mobile, the roadside sign becomes an inline compact sign with no hanging posts and no overflow.
- Keep machine paths explicit and crawlable.

The target is less narration and more place.
